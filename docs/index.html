How to play

Each turn, do one thing: Claim an empty cell next to your land (free, gain its resource 1–3 points) — but claiming a cell next to an enemy is contested ground and costs 2 points; Capture an adjacent enemy cell (costs 5 points, and you don't gain its resource); or Harvest (+3 points). First to control 39 of 64 cells (60%), or to eliminate all opponents, wins. Full rules in README.md; agent API in API.md.
